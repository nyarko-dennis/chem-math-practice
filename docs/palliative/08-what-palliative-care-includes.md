# What Palliative Care Includes

Palliative care addresses **four broad domains** of problems experienced by patients and families: physical, emotional/social, practical, and spiritual.

## 1. Physical Problems

Common symptoms or side effects of serious illness include:

- Pain
- Trouble sleeping
- Shortness of breath
- Loss of appetite
- Feeling sick to the stomach (nausea)

### Treatments may include:

- **Drugs** (analgesics, antiemetics, etc.)
- **Nutritional guidance**
- **Physical therapy**
- **Occupational therapy**
- **Integrative therapies** (e.g., massage, acupuncture)

## 2. Emotional, Social, and Coping Problems

Patients and their families face significant stress during illness, which can lead to:

- Fear
- Anxiety
- Hopelessness
- Depression

### Treatments may include:

- **Counseling**
- **Support groups**
- **Family meetings**
- **Referrals to mental health providers**

## 3. Practical Problems

Some of the problems brought on by illness are practical, such as:

- Money or job-related problems
- Insurance questions
- Legal issues
- Transportation
- Housing

### A palliative care team may:

- Explain complex medical forms or help families understand treatment choices
- Provide or refer families to **financial counseling**
- Help connect patients to resources for **transportation or housing**

## 4. Spiritual Issues

When people are challenged by illness, they may look for meaning or question their faith.

### A palliative care team may:

- Help patients and families explore their **beliefs and values**
- Support them in moving toward **acceptance and peace**
- Connect them with chaplains or appropriate religious figures (if desired)

## Holistic Care at a Glance

| Domain | Examples of Issues | Examples of Interventions |
|---|---|---|
| Physical | Pain, dyspnea, nausea, insomnia, anorexia | Analgesics, antiemetics, PT/OT, integrative therapies |
| Emotional/Social | Anxiety, depression, fear, hopelessness | Counseling, support groups, family meetings |
| Practical | Finances, insurance, housing, legal | Resource navigation, financial counseling |
| Spiritual | Meaning, faith, guilt, regret | Chaplaincy, exploration of beliefs |
