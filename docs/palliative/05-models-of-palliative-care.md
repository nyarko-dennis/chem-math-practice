# Models of Palliative Care

There are several clinical models that have demonstrated quality care for patients and families. They include a variety of disciplines that collaborate to provide quality care.

## 1. Home-Based Care

This is the **most common type** of palliative care delivery. It focuses on caring for the terminally ill **in their own home**.

It is the duty of the health worker to teach the caregiver how to manage the patient in the home (e.g., giving opioids on the clock, using the WHO analgesic ladder).

## 2. Outpatient Care

Terminally ill patients **report daily at the outpatient department** and palliative care is given.

Appointments may be scheduled on different days.

## 3. Outreach Services

A palliative team **moves from community to community** on scheduled dates when terminal patients gather for palliative care.

This includes both treatment and care of pain and other symptoms, with **referrals to specialists** when necessary.

## 4. Hospital-Based Care

A **palliative care team based in the hospital** goes round to various wards to attend to terminally ill patients.

## 5. Day Care

A facility is set up for terminally ill patients to be **cared for by trained personnel during the day**.

Patients go back to their various homes to sleep.

## 6. Hospice Inpatient Care

A facility is built **just for patients with terminal illness**. These patients, whose prognosis is months or less to death, are admitted and cared for by trained personnel.

## 7. Workplace Programme

Clients with terminal illness are **visited and given palliative treatment at their workplace**.

## Summary Table

| Model | Where care happens | Who delivers |
|---|---|---|
| Home-Based | Patient's home | Health worker teaches family caregiver |
| Outpatient | Outpatient clinic (daily) | Clinic palliative team |
| Outreach | Community gathering points | Mobile palliative team |
| Hospital-Based | Hospital wards | Hospital palliative team |
| Day Care | Day-care facility | Trained personnel (daytime only) |
| Hospice Inpatient | Dedicated hospice facility | Trained hospice personnel |
| Workplace | Patient's workplace | Visiting palliative team |
