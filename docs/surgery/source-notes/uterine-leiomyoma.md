## Slide 3

Definition
Uterine leiomyomas (fibroids) are benign smooth muscle tumors of the uterus.
They arise from the myometrium.
They are estrogen- and progesterone-dependent tumors.
Most common benign tumor in women of reproductive age.

## Slide 10

Pathophysiology
Initiation Phase: Mutation in a single smooth muscle cell → monoclonal tumor growth
Hormonal Stimulation: Estrogen → increases growth of fibroid cells. Progesterone → enhances proliferation and inhibits apoptosis
Cellular Proliferation: Smooth muscle cells multiply. Increased production of collagen and fibronectin.
Extracellular Matrix Deposition: Fibroids become firm and fibrotic.
Growth and Degeneration: Fibroids enlarge. May outgrow blood supply → degeneration (hyaline, cystic, red degeneration)

## Slide 24

Fate of Tubes and Ovaries
Rationale for Tube and Ovary Removal(Salpingo-oophorectomy): It’s usually indicated in post menopausal women, women who have no fertility wishes, risk of malignancy.
Rationale For Tube and Ovary Sparing: For the purpose of hormone preservation and prevention of early menopause. Indicated in women usually less than 45 years.
