## Slide 20

Rectal Prolapse Definition & Types
Rectal Prolapse is protrusion of part or all of the rectal wall through the anal opening.
Types
Mucosal prolapse
Only mucosa protrudes.
Complete prolapse
Entire rectal wall protrudes.
Internal prolapse
Rectum folds internally

## Slide 21

Straining
